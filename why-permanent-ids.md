# Why companies need a permanent ID, not just a name

Names are a poor way to identify a company. They collide, change and get spelled ten ways. Any system that matches companies by name, whether a CRM, a vendor list or a due-diligence workflow, will eventually merge two different companies or split one company into several.

## Name collisions and renamed companies

- **Collisions**: many unrelated businesses share names, across states and countries.
- **Renames**: a company changes its name after a merger or rebrand, and old records stop matching.
- **Spelling variants**: "Pvt Ltd", "Private Limited", abbreviations and typos create duplicates.

## What each registry number covers, and misses

| Identifier | Covers | Misses |
|---|---|---|
| CIN (India) | Companies registered with India's Ministry of Corporate Affairs | Firms outside India; many partnerships and proprietorships |
| LEI | Entities in financial transactions worldwide, if they have applied | Most small businesses never get one |
| UK company number | Companies registered at Companies House | Everything outside the UK |
| Wikidata ID | Notable organisations | Most ordinary companies |

No single number covers everyone, and one company may hold several.
